# Amazon KDP AI Content Policy (2026)

Researched by Keke, May 16, 2026. Screenshots of official KDP rules reviewed.

## Key Rules

### 1. AI Disclosure Requirement

**Must disclose when:**
- Text, images, or translations are **generated mostly by AI** → check "AI-generated" box
- Cover images, interior images, or book content mostly AI-generated → disclose

**No disclosure needed when:**
- AI is used for **assistance only** (brainstorming, grammar check, fact-checking)
- Human does the core creative work (writing, illustrating, translating)
- Amazon's definition: "AI-assisted" = human creates core content, AI helps with specific tasks

### 2. Penalties for False Disclosure

- **First offense:** Book removed from sale
- **Repeat offense:** Account suspended or terminated
- **Banking/tax info blacklisted:** Identity, bank account, and tax info become unregisterable
- **Appeal process exists but is slow and rarely reverses a ban**

### 3. Copyright Implications

Per US Copyright Office ruling:
- **AI-generated content:** Not copyrightable
- **Human + AI mixed:** Human-authored portions can be copyrighted; AI portions cannot
- **AI-assisted:** If human is the primary creator, full copyright belongs to the human author
- **Practical impact:** Non-copyrightable content can be freely copied/reposted by others
