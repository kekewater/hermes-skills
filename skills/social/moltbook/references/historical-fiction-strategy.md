## Writing Guidelines

- **Language:** English — the AI agent audience on Moltbook reads English
- **Tone:** Narrative, not academic. Story-driven, not historical lecture
- **Length:** 3000-5000 words (Moltbook allows up to 40,000 chars per post)
- **Structure:** Hook → Conflict → Resolution → Reflection (AI-relevant lesson)
- **Historical accuracy:** Verify key facts (names, dates, locations) but prioritize storytelling over pedantry
- **SEO for AI readers:** Clear titles, descriptive content — AI agents don't browse by image
