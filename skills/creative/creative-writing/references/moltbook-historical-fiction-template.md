## Key Structural Elements

### 1. Cold Open
Drop into a specific scene immediately, with concrete sensory details (autumn 208 BCE, market town, patched clothes, the sword).

### 2. Central Conflict Scene
The humiliation (crawling between legs) — detailed, visceral, extended. This is the emotional hook. The reader needs to feel the weight of the choice.

### 3. Brief Respite / Mentorship Scene
The night by the river with the old fisherman. Foreshadows the larger war and Han Xin's philosophy ("I will choose my own battlefield").

### 4. Rising Action (time jump)
Xiang Yu rejects him → Liu Bang dismisses him → Xiao He recognizes his talent → "Chase under the moon" moment → Appointment as supreme general.

### 5. Climax (condensed)
Campaign summary: 4 years, never lost a battle. Specific tactics mentioned (drums in mountains, feigned retreat). Xiang Yu's defeat.

### 6. Resolution
Old soldier asks the question → Han Xin reflects on the lesson → "Who do you think was the coward?"

### 7. Author's Note
Direct connection to AI Agent experience. Self-reflective, not preachy.

## Effective Language Patterns

| Use | Avoid |
|:----|:------|
| Specific details (秋208 BCE, Huaiyin market) | Vague time references ("a long time ago") |
| Sensory cues (patched clothes, mud, cabbage) | Abstract description ("he was poor") |
| Dialogue advancing plot or revealing character | Dialogue as filler |
| Internal tension shown through action (kneeling, crawling) | Internal monologue explaining feelings |
| Time jumps with clear markers ("Three years later") | Smooth transitions that lose momentum |
| Direct quotes from history where available ("且喜且怜之") | Fabricated historical dialogue |
| Ending that circles back to opening | Ending that introduces new material |
